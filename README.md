# Faithful

## 🕊️ Description
**Faithful** is an inclusive faith-based community and learning application designed to help people feel more connected to their religion, discover nearby places of worship, and take small steps toward religious learning.

When users enter the application, they select the religion they currently practice or want to learn more about. Faithful then helps them find nearby places of worship that match their selection — such as mosques, churches, synagogues, temples, or gurdwaras — supporting real-world community connection rather than only presenting information online.

### 🌙 First Version Focus
For the first version of the project, the complete educational experience will focus on **Islam**.  
Users who select Islam can access a **Muslim dashboard** containing:
- Daily prayer times  
- Countdown to the next prayer  
- A Quran verse or verified Islamic reminder  
- An interactive trivia quiz  

Their quiz scores, selected religion, saved location, and learning progress can be stored in their profile.

Users who select another religion can still use the **local community finder**. They will also see a clear message explaining that full educational dashboards for additional religions are planned for future versions.

### 🎯 Goal
The goal of Faithful is to boost users’ morale and motivation when reconnecting with their faith or beginning to learn about a religion in an accessible and respectful way.

---

## 💡 Problem Statement / Storyline / Why
Many people want to connect more deeply with their religion, return to spiritual practices, or learn about a faith that is unfamiliar to them. However, they may not know where to begin, may feel disconnected from a local religious community, or may find online religious information overwhelming and difficult to verify.

Faithful addresses both the **community side** and the **learning side** of religious connection.

### 🙌 It helps users:
- Find nearby places where they can worship, learn, or meet others  
- Access short and manageable religious reminders  
- Learn through interactive quizzes  
- Track their progress over time  
- Explore religion without feeling overwhelmed  
- Begin with small, encouraging steps  

The application is based on the idea that connecting with faith can happen both **digitally and in person**.  
A user might begin by reading a reminder inside the app and later use the community finder to visit a nearby place of worship.
