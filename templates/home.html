Community Guidelines

Faithful is a respectful space for learning about faith, discovering local communities, and building positive connections.

Respect every faith

Treat all religions, traditions, and community members with dignity and respect.

Use information responsibly

Faithful provides educational information and does not replace guidance from qualified religious leaders.

Protect your privacy

Only provide the location information needed to find communities near you.

Keep the community safe

Do not use Faithful to promote harassment, hatred, misinformation, or discrimination.
